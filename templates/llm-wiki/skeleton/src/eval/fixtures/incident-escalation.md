## Summary

A Sev-1 pages the on-call engineer first. Escalation is time-based, so no
acknowledgement is also an answer.

## Details

The on-call engineer has 10 minutes to acknowledge. Unacknowledged, the page
escalates to the secondary on-call, and 10 minutes after that to the
engineering manager, who owns the decision to open an incident channel.
